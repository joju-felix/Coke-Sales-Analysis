# 🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Analysis | 2021

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Coca-Cola beverage sales data for the year 2021 using **Microsoft Excel**. The objective is to evaluate sales performance, identify revenue and profitability trends, compare brand and regional performance, and uncover opportunities for business improvement.

The analysis uses sales data from multiple retailers across different geographical regions in the United States.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify the highest and lowest revenue-generating beverage brands.
- Compare sales performance across geographical regions.
- Evaluate operating profit and operating margins.
- Analyse monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Identify business opportunities to improve sales and profitability.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains 3,888 sales records covering January to December 2021.

**Key variables include:**

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Retailer | Retailer name |

| Price per Unit | Selling price per unit |

| Operating Profit | Profit generated |

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Brand Performance
- Coca-Cola generated the highest revenue, approximately $2.02 million.
- Dasani Water ranked second in revenue.
- Fanta recorded the lowest revenue among the six beverage brands.
- Coca-Cola also led in operating profit.

### 2. Regional Performance
- The West region generated the highest revenue, approximately $2.84 million.
- The Midwest recorded the lowest revenue, approximately $1.15 million.
- The South region achieved the highest operating margin at approximately 41.97%.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trends
- December recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $1,045,775.
- March recorded the lowest monthly revenue at approximately $483,530.
- Sales increased significantly from May to July.
- Revenue declined between August and October before recovering in November and December.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Strengthen marketing and sales strategies for high-performing beverage brands.
- Investigate opportunities to improve the performance of lower-revenue brands.
- Identify factors contributing to the South region's higher operating margin.
- Use monthly sales trends to improve seasonal sales planning and inventory allocation.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis highlights significant differences in brand performance, regional profitability, and monthly sales trends.

Coca-Cola emerged as the leading revenue-generating brand, the West region contributed the highest overall revenue, and the South achieved the highest operating margin. December was the strongest sales month of 2021.

These findings demonstrate how spreadsheet-based data analysis can support business decision-making through meaningful insights and performance evaluation.
